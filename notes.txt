Q1. What is NetSuite and how is it different from other ERPs?
A1.ERP is the idea; NetSuite is a cloud implementation of that idea — it doesn’t replace ERP, it is an ERP system 
(but modern, integrated, and on the cloud).

Q2.What are the main modules available in NetSuite?
A2.NetSuite offers modules for Finance, CRM, Inventory, HR, Projects, and E-Commerce — all customizable through SuiteScript, 
SuiteBuilder, and SuiteTalk integration tools.
